About Us

The Story of DRYBEA MARKET

Sri Lankan Traditional Seafood Heritage

Based in the coastal heart of Tangalle, Sri Lanka, DRYBEA MARKET is committed to preserving the authentic flavors of our southern culinary heritage. We specialize in producing and supplying premium quality dried fish, Maldive fish chips, and authentic seafood condiments.

Our mission is to bring the freshest, most hygienic, and highly nutritious seafood products directly to your dining table. Using sun-drying techniques perfected over 100 years and modern vacuum packaging, we ensure zero compromises on quality, taste, or safety.

  • 100% Natural Ingredients
  • No Artificial Preservatives
  • Sustainable Seafood Sourcing
